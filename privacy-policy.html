Privacy Policy

LinkedIn Comment Assistant

Last updated: October 27, 2026

1. Overview

This workflow assists with preparing, reviewing, and tracking suggested comments on LinkedIn posts. This policy describes the information that may be processed when the workflow is used.

2. Information Processed

Information may include LinkedIn post URLs and text, author information supplied by the user, AI-generated comments, approval decisions, processing statuses, and email addresses used for notifications.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used to analyze submitted posts, generate comment suggestions, check comment quality, record approval decisions, prevent duplicate processing, and send workflow notifications.

4. Third-Party Services

The workflow may use n8n for automation, an AI service to generate comments, and Gmail to deliver previews and notifications. Information may be processed by these services as needed to perform their functions, subject to their applicable terms and privacy policies.

5. LinkedIn Access

The current workflow accepts post information manually and provides suggested comments for review. It does not currently publish comments through an automated LinkedIn API integration. Any future integration will depend on the permissions granted and the implementation used.

6. Data Storage and Retention

Workflow records may be stored in a configured n8n Data Table to track decisions and prevent duplicate processing. The operator should define and communicate appropriate retention and deletion procedures.

7. Security

Access to workflow data and credentials should be restricted to authorized users. Reasonable safeguards should be used to protect information against unauthorized access or disclosure.
